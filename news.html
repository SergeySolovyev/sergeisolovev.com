May 27, 2026 · revert.pro

REVERT went live on Ethereum mainnet — bytecode-level contract risk scoring

Public service at revert.pro scoring the roughly 95% of Ethereum mainnet that ships unverified, where source-based tools return nothing. XGBoost over EVM bytecode features, trained on 117,091 Slither-labelled contracts; F1 0.948 agreement with Slither, 95% CI [0.944, 0.951]. Mar 26, 2026 · figshare

Preprint on limit order book mid-price prediction — dual-branch BiGRU-CNN

Domain-aware dual-branch recurrent network for high-frequency mid-price prediction, posted to figshare under CC BY 4.0. Weighted Pearson 0.266 across 12,165 sequences over 12.1 million timesteps from the Wunder Fund RNN challenge; it beats LightGBM on 58.3% of sequences. Two negative findings are reported: feature sufficiency, and degradation from ensembling.
